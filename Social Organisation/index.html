About Us

Your Impact Begins with Thaagam Foundation

At Thaagam Foundation, we're committed to creating a meaningful and lasting impact that transcends the ordinary. Our passion for humanitarian aid, community empowerment, and individual dignity drives our mission. Our initiatives are carefully crafted to address immediate needs and foster sustainable development. In addition to our extensive programs, we're equally devoted to ensuring your involvement is impactful and fulfilling. Our platform is designed with your convenience in mind, offering secure donations and a responsive support team to assist you every step of the way

Our Mission

Social change organizations

These organizations aim to change entrenched ideas, dismantle oppressive systems of power, and find solutions to problems. They do this by developing strategies, supporting social justice values, and conducting organizational assessments.

Community social organizations

These organizations help communities achieve their desired results for individuals and families. They help people demonstrate resiliency in the face of adversity.

Businesses with social missions

These businesses have a social mission that drives their decisions and communicates their values to customers, employees, and stakeholders. For example, CommonBond partners with Pencils of Promise to provide schools and technology to young students in developing countries. TOMS donates $1 for every $3 it makes in net annual profits to various nonprofit organizations.
